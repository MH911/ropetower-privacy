Rope Tower – Privacy Policy

Developer: Quantum Play Studio · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Rope Tower ("the game") uses and why. Contact: Meetharia57@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your player name, avatar, settings, best height, diamonds, unlocked items and achievements are stored on your device so the game remembers your progress. You can play without any account.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud save

If you choose Sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to store your Google account name, email address, a user ID, and your game progress (player name, avatar, best height, diamonds, unlocked items and achievements). We also store your country, taken from your phone's region setting (no location permission is used). This lets you restore your progress on another device. This data is sent over an encrypted connection.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded video ads. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about how you use the app to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Learn how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional diamond packs. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. We receive a confirmation of the purchase so the game can add your diamonds.

Sharing your tower

When you tap Share your tower, the game creates a picture of your tower and height and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share with.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your account and data

If you signed in, you can delete your account and all cloud data inside the game: open Settings › Account and cloud save › Delete account. You can also email Meetharia57@gmail.com with the subject "Rope Tower data deletion" and the email address you signed in with; we will delete your account and cloud data within 30 days. Progress stored only on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
